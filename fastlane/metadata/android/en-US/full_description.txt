ClashFest is a rule-based proxy / VPN client for Android built on the mihomo (Clash.Meta) core.

<b>Made for the common case</b>: paste or scan a subscription link, tap Connect, done. The subscription stays untouched; everything you change in the app lives in a separate layer on top of it.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>VLESS (REALITY, XHTTP, Vision), VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, TUIC, WireGuard, SSH, mieru and more, via the mihomo core</li>
<li>Subscriptions in Clash YAML format with headers from your operator (quota, expiry, announcements, branding)</li>
<li>Per-app routing (allow-list / block-list), rule sets, custom rules, DNS and hosts editor, tunnels</li>
<li>Proxy groups inline on the home screen: pick a node, test latency of one node or a whole group</li>
<li>Live connections and logs</li>
<li>Hardened by default: local SOCKS/HTTP listeners are closed, geo downloads only from trusted mirrors</li>
<li>QR scanner without Google services</li>
<li>Remote control of a TV box on the same network</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>: no analytics, no tracking, no developer server. Your traffic goes to the servers of the operator you chose.
